## Resolved decisions (locked 2026-05-20)

1. **Email signature — minimal, no phone, no calendar link inline.** Calendar lives in the body of email 2/3 as a soft CTA, not in the sig. Phone numbers in cold-email sigs scream "vendor." Use:

   ```
   Steve Betancur
   Founder, ThermaShift
   thermashift.net
   ```

   For Email 1 specifically: drop the title line entirely. Just "Steve Betancur \\ ThermaShift". Reads more peer-to-peer.

2. **Calendar — Cal.com free tier.** Lifetime free, no rate-limit on first hits, clean UX, no Calendly brand stink. Steve sets up a 15-min slot named "ThermaShift intro call" at `cal.com/steve-betancur/15min` (or chosen handle). For the first week, the fallback "reply with a time that works in your TZ and I'll send an invite" is equally fine — zero infrastructure required and feels more human. Recommend: set up Cal.com NOW so the link is ready, but don't include it in Email 1. Add it only if/when prospects reply asking.

3. **Reply handling — Steve handles round 1 personally.** AI Closer (Vapi/Alex) does NOT auto-engage on cold replies. First impressions matter too much. Once Steve has replied to the first message and context is established, the AI Closer can take over follow-up scheduling, qualification, demo coordination — but only after the human-anchored first exchange. This is durable policy, not just initial-batch policy.

4. **Day-1 first batch (5 contacts):** Mark Taylor (EdgeConneX, 82) · Tye Stromer (Novva, 82) · Wayne Watson (Applied Digital, 78) · Mike Kinsey (NTT Global, 77) · Patrick Snider (DataBank, 75). Highest scores, cleanest Tier-A operator fit. Note: Novva now has a second contact (Hiroshi Inzunza) in a newer BJ list — **only send to Tye, not both**. Same applies to DataBank (3 contacts in pool) — only Patrick Snider for batch 1.

5. **Send time — Tue/Wed/Thu at 10:00 AM recipient local time.** Avoid Mon (inbox-clearing) and Fri (weekend mode). 10am hits after the morning standup wave but before lunch fatigue. BrandJet's per-contact TZ inference handles the localization. **NOT 9:30am** — too close to standup hour for ops folks. **NOT 11am** — too close to lunch buffer. 10am ± 15min spread looks human, not robotic. Spread the batch of 5 across Tue + Wed of launch week (3 + 2) so we're not stacking all sends in one window.
